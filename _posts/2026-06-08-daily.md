## 2. Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis Discloses GenAI Use in Development

- **title**: Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis Discloses GenAI Use in Development
- **title_zh**: 《古墓丽影：亚特兰蒂斯遗产》披露开发中使用生成式AI
- **summary**: Crystal Dynamics disclosed that Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis used generative AI tools during an "early exploration" phase of development, with AI-assisted assets either replaced or refined by humans. The studio emphasized that all finished content remains human-crafted. This disclosure comes amid growing industry tension, as major titles like ARC Raiders, Crimson Desert, and Clair Obscur have successfully used GenAI tools despite public backlash. The critical insight is that player purchasing behavior shows no meaningful correlation with GenAI disclosure—commercially successful games continue to use these tools regardless of controversy. This suggests the industry is approaching a tipping point where GenAI integration becomes normalized in AAA pipelines, with disclosure becoming a risk-management formality rather than a genuine creative constraint.
- **summary_zh**: Crystal Dynamics确认《古墓丽影：亚特兰蒂斯遗产》在"早期探索"阶段使用了生成式AI工具，同时强调所有最终呈现的内容均由人工创作完成。这一披露发生在行业对AI使用日益敏感的背景下——此前ARC Raiders、Crimson Desert和Clair Obscur等大作均因使用GenAI引发争议，但并未影响其商业成功。关键洞察在于：玩家购买行为与AI披露之间没有显著相关性，商业成功与是否使用AI工具基本无关。这表明行业可能正在接近一个临界点——GenAI整合将在AAA级游戏管线中常态化，而披露声明正逐渐沦为风险管理的形式，而非真正的创作约束。对于开发者而言，这意味着AI工具的使用策略将更多取决于内部效率考量，而非外部舆论压力。

## 4. Sega Confirms Generative AI Use in Crazy Taxi: World Tour

- **title**: Sega Confirms Generative AI Use in Crazy Taxi: World Tour
- **title_zh**: 世嘉确认《疯狂出租车：世界之旅》使用生成式AI
- **summary**: Sega confirmed that Crazy Taxi: World Tour, announced at the Xbox Games Showcase, used generative AI as a support tool for background asset development, with all AI-generated content subject to human review. The company explicitly stated no AI was used for performers in the game. This follows a pattern where major publishers are increasingly transparent about AI use while carefully delineating boundaries—AI for environmental assets, humans for character performance. The significance is in the normalization of AI disclosure as a standard practice: Sega's response is measured, emphasizing AI as an "optional support tool" that lets developers "focus more on creative tasks." This framing suggests the industry is converging on a compromise position where AI handles production bottlenecks (background art, localization) while human creators retain control over narrative and performance elements, potentially establishing a template for future AI usage policies across the industry.
- **summary_zh**: 世嘉在Xbox游戏展示会上公布的《疯狂出租车：世界之旅》确认使用生成式AI作为背景资产开发的支持工具，同时强调所有AI生成内容均经过人工审核，且游戏中的表演者未使用AI。这延续了大型发行商日益透明的AI使用披露模式，同时谨慎划分了AI与人工创作的边界——AI负责环境资产，人类负责角色表演。其深层意义在于AI披露正成为行业标准化实践：世嘉将AI定位为"可选支持工具"，称其帮助开发者"更专注于创意任务"。这种表述暗示行业正在形成一种妥协共识——AI处理生产瓶颈（背景美术、本地化），而人类创作者保留对叙事和表演元素的控制权。这可能为未来整个游戏行业的AI使用政策建立模板，也可能成为应对玩家和工会压力的一种公关策略。值得注意的是，粉丝对此已表达不满，表明即使"有限度"的AI使用仍面临舆论挑战。
