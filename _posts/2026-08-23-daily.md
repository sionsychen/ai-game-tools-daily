## [China’s Gaming Market Tests the AI-Native Promise](https://www.thinkchina.sg/technology/game-studios-have-bought-ai-players-still-arent-sold)

The piece argues that the industry is confusing adoption with innovation: thousands of AI-labeled demos prove the tooling works, but players treat the flood as a signal of lower quality. The most useful test of an AI-native game is whether removing the AI leaves any game at all.

## [十二人团队拒绝生成式 AI，自研工具挑战 3A 级规模](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/the-hollywood-disruptors-making-a-large-scale-game-with-a-12-person-team-and-no-generative-ai)

title: "A 12-Person Studio Bets on Proprietary Tools, Not Generative AI"
title_zh: "十二人团队拒绝生成式 AI，自研工具挑战 3A 级规模"
summary: "Wolf Haus Games is building a large co-op open-world title with 12 people by investing in scalable internal tooling, deliberately avoiding generative AI as its scaling lever."
summary_zh: "Wolf Haus Games 仅用 12 人开发大型开放世界合作游戏，通过早期投入可扩展、单人可调试的内部工具链实现高保真内容量产。团队明确回避生成式 AI，将竞争力押注在流程与工具创新上，这为独立工作室如何在资源受限下追求大规模项目提供了另一种技术路径。其经验表明，一套可复用的自研工具链能将创作复杂度从人力线性扩展转化为系统化复用，从而改变独立游戏的成本结构。"
sourceUrl: "https://www.gamesindustry.biz/the-hollywood-disruptors-making-a-large-scale-game-with-a-12-person-team-and-no-generative-ai"
category: ai-tools
---

Wolf Haus Games’ approach treats tooling as the core product. By building systems that one person can debug and scale, the studio keeps a tiny team while matching much larger productions. The lesson is that workflow, not generative output, may be the real force multiplier for small teams.
