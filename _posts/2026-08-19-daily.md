## 1. Generative AI Reshapes Game Creation via Specialized Pipelines

title: Generative AI Reshapes Game Creation via Specialized Pipelines  
title_zh: 生成式AI通过细分管线重塑游戏创作  
summary: Tools such as Unity Muse, Roblox Code Assist, and EA's test-triage research show generative AI is being adopted as narrow, inspectable helpers rather than autonomous creators, keeping humans in control of direction and quality.
