  <article class="article-card" id="article-1">
    <h3><a href="https://quasa.io/media/roblox-unveils-build-create-games-from-simple-text-prompts-with-ai" target="_blank">Roblox Build Brings Text-to-Game Creation to Mobile</a></h3>
    <p class="meta">Source: quasa.io | Category: ai-tools</p>
    <p>Roblox's new mobile-first tool Build turns text prompts into playable game prototypes directly inside the app. It generates core mechanics, environments, characters, and audio, and syncs with Roblox Studio for further editing. The alpha begins July 28 in New Zealand. Roblox argues quality will be enforced by its retention-based discovery algorithm, but developers worry about "AI slop" flooding the platform. The broader strategy includes AI agents in Studio and the Cube 3D foundation model.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="article-card" id="article-2">
    <h3><a href="https://www.pokgaigamer.com/p/using-ai-to-make-a-game-from-scratch" target="_blank">Solo AI Game Dev in 2026: A Reality Check</a></h3>
    <p class="meta">Source: pokgaigamer.com | Category: ai-trends</p>
    <p>A Hong Kong indie developer tested Grok, Midjourney, Unity AI tools, and Suno to build a game from scratch. The verdict: AI is excellent for brainstorming, concept art, music, and rapid prototyping, but falls apart on coherent full-game code, consistent art style, and polished game feel. Legal and copyright issues remain unresolved. The practical takeaway is to use AI for tedious parts while investing in core development skills.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="article-card" id="article-3">
    <h3><a href="https://www.soonlab.ai/blog/best-game-engines" target="_blank">Choosing Game Engines in the AI-Assisted Era</a></h3>
    <p class="meta">Source: soonlab.ai | Category: ai-trends</p>
    <p>A 2026 guide for beginners and indie developers frames engine selection around AI-assisted workflows. It recommends Godot for lightweight 2D projects and Unity for 3D, while noting that AI coding, debugging, sprite generation, and procedural content creation are now part of the decision criteria. The core argument is that matching project goals and workflow style matters more than chasing the most powerful engine.</p>
  </article>
