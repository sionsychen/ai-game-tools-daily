## Godot Foundation Bans AI-Generated Code Contributions

**title:** Godot Foundation Bans AI-Generated Code Contributions
**title_zh:** Godot基金会全面禁止AI生成代码贡献
**summary:** The Godot Foundation has announced a sweeping ban on AI-generated code contributions, citing an overwhelming flood of low-quality "vibe-coded" pull requests that have become "demoralizing" for maintainers. The new policy prohibits autonomous AI agent use, requires all code to be human-authored, and bans AI-generated text in human-to-human communications. This marks one of the strongest anti-AI stances in the open-source game development community. The move reflects growing frustration across the industry with AI-generated contributions that lack accountability—maintainers noted they "can't trust heavy users of AI to understand their code enough to fix it." While AI assistance for "menial things" like code completion remains permitted, the policy signals a broader pushback against unregulated AI infiltration into critical development infrastructure.
**summary_zh:** Godot基金会近期宣布全面禁止AI生成的代码贡献，这一决定源于大量低质量"vibe-coded"拉取请求对维护者造成的严重负担。新政策明确禁止自主AI代理参与、要求所有代码必须由人类编写，并禁止在人际沟通中使用AI生成文本。这是开源游戏开发社区中最强硬的反AI立场之一，反映出整个行业对AI生成内容缺乏责任归属的深层焦虑。维护者直言"无法信任重度AI用户足够理解自己的代码以进行修复"。虽然代码补全等"琐碎辅助"仍被允许，但这一政策标志着开源社区开始对AI无限制渗透关键开发基础设施进行系统性反击。对于独立开发者而言，这意味着依赖AI自动生成核心代码的时代在Godot生态中正式结束，人类开发者的技术深度和责任感重新被置于首位。

## Game Oracle Research Reveals AI Disclosure Cuts Sales by 40-60%

**title:** Game Oracle Research Reveals AI Disclosure Cuts Sales by 40-60%
**title_zh:** Game Oracle研究显示AI披露标签导致销量暴跌40%-60%
**summary:** New research from Game Oracle provides the first concrete data showing that Steam's mandatory AI disclosure policy is severely impacting commercial performance. The study found that established developers using AI face a "catastrophic" 40-60% drop in sales compared to similar projects without AI disclosures, while AI-labeled games receive approximately 53% fewer user reviews. The data reveals a stark paradox: AI reduces development time (from ~14 days to ~10 days for mobile games), but the "AI stigma" disproportionately punishes high-quality studios while barely affecting low-quality titles. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney has called Steam's AI labels a "Scarlet Letter" that unfairly harms developers. The findings force a critical industry question: if AI tools genuinely improve efficiency, why does transparency about their use trigger such severe market penalties?
**summary_zh:** Game Oracle发布的最新研究首次用数据证实了Steam强制AI披露政策对商业表现的严重冲击。研究显示，使用AI的成熟开发商面临"灾难性"的40%-60%销量下滑，AI标签游戏获得的用户评价数量减少约53%。数据揭示了一个尖锐悖论：AI确实缩短了开发周期（移动端游戏从约14天缩短至10天），但"AI污名"却不成比例地惩罚高质量工作室，而对低质量作品几乎无影响。Epic CEO Tim Sweeney将Steam的AI标签称为伤害开发者的"红字"。这一发现迫使行业直面核心问题：如果AI工具真能提高效率，为何透明披露其使用会引发如此严重的市场惩罚？对于正在考虑是否采用AI工具的开发者来说，这项研究意味着必须在效率增益与潜在的商业风险之间做出艰难权衡。

## How AI 3D Tools Are Reshaping Indie Game Prototyping

**title:** How AI 3D Tools Are Reshaping Indie Game Prototyping
**title_zh:** AI 3D工具如何重塑独立游戏原型开发
**summary:** A detailed analysis of Meshy AI's role in indie game development reveals how AI 3D generators are transforming the prototyping pipeline. Meshy-6, launched in January 2026, now ships watertight meshes by default—a critical improvement that eliminates the manual repair step required by previous versions. In a 2026 blind test involving 1,331 senior artists from NetEase and Tencent, Meshy-6 won 63.8% of comparisons against Tripo 3.1. The tool enables developers to move from text prompt to textured, rigged character in under five minutes. However, the analysis emphasizes that AI 3D tools remain a "block-out and ideation layer" rather than a replacement for human artists—hero characters, facial detail, and complex animation still require manual refinement. At $20/month for ~50 finished assets, the cost is lower than a single freelance commission, making it accessible for two-person indie teams.
**summary_zh:** 对Meshy AI在独立游戏开发中角色的深入分析显示，AI 3D生成器正在根本性改变原型制作流程。2026年1月发布的Meshy-6默认输出水密网格，这一关键改进消除了此前版本必需的手动修复步骤。在涉及1331名网易和腾讯资深艺术家的2026年盲测中，Meshy-6以63.8%的胜率击败Tripo 3.1。该工具使开发者能在五分钟内从文本提示生成带纹理、已绑定的角色。然而分析强调，AI 3D工具目前仍是"粗模和概念层"而非人类艺术家的替代品——主角角色、面部细节和复杂动画仍需人工精修。每月20美元可获得约50个成品资产，成本低于单次外包委托，对两人独立团队极具吸引力。这一工具链的成熟标志着独立游戏开发进入"快速验证、人工精修"的新范式。
