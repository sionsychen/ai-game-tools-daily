## [Meta's Pocket App Turns Prompts Into Shareable Mobile Games](https://www.chatai.com/posts/meta-s-new-pocket-app-turns-ai-prompts-into-interactive-games)

Pocket extends Meta's "vibe coding" experiments from images and video into playable interactive software. By making gizmos remixable and feed-distributed, Meta is betting that AI-generated games will spread like memes. The open question is whether the output will be novel entertainment or just more disposable content.
