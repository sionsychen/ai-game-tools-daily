## Article 3
- **title**: AI Boom's Seismic Impact on Video Game Hardware Supply Chain
- **title_zh**: AI热潮对游戏硬件供应链的地震级影响
- **summary**: The AI data center boom is causing severe hardware shortages and price spikes for consumer gaming components. DDR5 RAM now costs 6-8x more than in September 2025, as chip producers like Samsung, Hynix, and Micron pivot from consumer DRAM to high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI servers. The shortage cascades through GPUs—Nvidia isn't providing GDDR7 VRAM to board manufacturers, forcing Asus, Gigabyte, and MSI to source memory themselves in a volatile spot market. SSDs are next, as AI data centers compete for NAND raw materials. Sony has already raised PS5 prices by $100, while Nintendo faces ~40% DDR5 cost increases for Switch 2. This supply chain disruption creates a paradox: AI tools promise to make game development cheaper, but the hardware required to run them is becoming prohibitively expensive.
- **summary_zh**: AI数据中心热潮正在导致消费级游戏组件的严重短缺和价格飙升。DDR5内存价格较2025年9月上涨6-8倍，因为三星、海力士和美光等芯片生产商从消费级DRAM转向为AI服务器生产高带宽内存（HBM）。短缺效应波及GPU——英伟达不再向板卡制造商提供GDDR7显存，迫使华硕、技嘉和微星在波动的现货市场自行采购内存。SSD是下一个受影响的品类，因为AI数据中心正在争夺NAND原材料。索尼已将PS5价格提高100美元，而任天堂面临Switch 2约40%的DDR5成本上涨。这种供应链中断创造了一个悖论：AI工具承诺降低游戏开发成本，但运行这些工具所需的硬件正变得令人望而却步。对于独立开发者和小型工作室而言，这意味着进入门槛可能因硬件成本上升而提高，尽管AI工具本身正在降低技术门槛。

It also raises questions about content moderation, IP ownership, and quality control in AI-generated narrative spaces.
