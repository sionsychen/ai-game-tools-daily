## Article 1
- **title**: GDC 2026 Report: AI Backlash, Burnout Crisis & Co-Development Shift
- **title_zh**: GDC 2026报告：AI反弹、开发者倦怠危机与联合开发转型
- **summary**: The GDC 2026 Trends Report reveals a critical industry infrastructure crisis where 52% of developers now view generative AI as actively harmful, up from 30% in 2025. The backlash is fiercest among creative roles—64% of artists, 63% of designers, and 59% of programmers oppose corporate AI integration over job security and artistic integrity concerns. Compounding this, 94% of developers report burnout symptoms and only 20% feel they have good mental health. Studios are pivoting to co-development partnerships to share costs, with 6% of employees now at dedicated co-dev companies. The report signals that the industry's challenge isn't finding better AI tools, but repairing the broken human and financial infrastructure that keeps the workforce stable.
- **summary_zh**: GDC 2026趋势报告揭示了游戏行业面临的严重基础设施危机：52%的开发者认为生成式AI正在损害行业，较2025年的30%大幅上升。创意岗位的反弹最为激烈——64%的视觉/技术美术、63%的游戏设计师和59%的程序员强烈反对企业AI整合，主要出于对工作安全和艺术完整性的担忧。更令人担忧的是，94%的开发者报告出现倦怠症状，仅20%认为自己的心理健康状况良好。为应对压力，工作室正转向联合开发模式分担成本，目前有6%的员工在专门的联合开发公司工作。报告指出，行业面临的真正挑战不是寻找更先进的AI工具，而是修复维持劳动力稳定所需的破碎的人力与财务基础设施。这一趋势表明，AI技术的快速采用与开发者福祉之间的鸿沟正在扩大，可能迫使行业重新评估其技术采纳策略。

## Article 2
- **title**: HoYoverse Invests $14.7B to Build In-House AI Ecosystem for Game Development
- **title_zh**: 米哈游投资147亿美元打造内部AI生态系统用于游戏开发
- **summary**: HoYoverse co-founder and CEO Liu Wei revealed plans to invest up to ¥100 billion ($14.7 billion) over three years to cultivate in-house AI capabilities, moving beyond external AI models. The investment targets AI application architecture, training systems, GPU clusters, and advanced NPC systems. This signals a major shift for the Genshin Impact and Honkai: Star Rail developer, which has earned nearly $10 billion on mobile alone. The AI tech will debut in Petit Planet, an upcoming life simulator, and a large-scale Unreal Engine 5 fantasy project. This massive investment reflects a broader trend of top-tier studios building proprietary AI infrastructure rather than relying on third-party models, potentially creating competitive moats around their development pipelines.
- **summary_zh**: 米哈游联合创始人兼CEO刘伟透露，计划在未来三年内投资高达1000亿元人民币（147亿美元）培养内部AI能力，超越对外部AI模型的依赖。投资重点包括AI应用架构、训练系统、GPU集群和高级NPC系统。这标志着《原神》和《崩坏：星穹铁道》开发商的重大战略转变——这两款游戏仅在移动端就创造了近100亿美元收入。AI技术将首次应用于即将推出的生活模拟游戏《Petit Planet》以及一个大型虚幻引擎5奇幻项目。如此巨额投资反映了顶级工作室建设专有AI基础设施而非依赖第三方模型的更广泛趋势，可能在其开发流程周围建立竞争护城河。这也表明，拥有足够资本的工作室正试图通过控制AI技术栈来保持竞争优势，而不是与竞争对手共享相同的工具集。
