    title_zh: "谷歌SynthID AI水印技术获OpenAI、英伟达等巨头采用"
    summary: "Ars Technica reports that Google's SynthID AI watermarking technology is expanding beyond Google's own ecosystem to major industry players including OpenAI, Nvidia, Kakao, and ElevenLabs. The invisible digital watermark is embedded directly into the pixels of AI-generated images and videos and into the waveforms of AI audio, making it resistant to compression, cropping, and rotation. Google says SynthID has already labeled 100 billion images and videos plus 60,000 years' worth of audio. For game developers and asset marketplaces, the widespread adoption of a unified watermarking standard could provide a crucial tool for verifying asset provenance and distinguishing human-created from AI-generated content."
