**title:** Sony Leadership Calls AI "Foundational Technology" for PlayStation Strategy
**title_zh:** 索尼高层称AI是PlayStation战略的"基础技术"
**summary:** Sony Interactive Entertainment leadership has explicitly labeled AI as an "important foundational technology supporting our strategy," detailing practical applications across first-party studios. Sony is also experimenting with "AI-first initiatives" at a fundamental level while maintaining that "the vision, design, and emotional impact of our games will always come from the talent of our studios." This statement comes alongside Sony's controversial decision to end physical disc production by January 2028, signaling a fully digital, AI-augmented future for PlayStation.

**title:** AI-Native Games Survey Defines the Future of Playable AI
**title_zh:** AI原生游戏调查定义可玩AI的未来
**summary:** A new arXiv survey paper titled "AI-Native Games: A Survey and Roadmap" establishes a rigorous definition for games where generative AI is constitutive of the core gameplay loop—not merely a production tool. The researchers analyzed 53 publicly available AI-native games and prototypes, introducing a G/N taxonomy that classifies games by player-facing type (G) and dominant AI mechanic (N). The corpus reveals concentration around language-forward designs (narrative adventure, epistemic interaction) while categories like semantic adjudication, multi-agent simulation, and generative construction remain underexplored. The paper argues that the central design challenge is "organizing semantic openness into stable gameplay"—a critical insight for developers building AI-driven experiences. This academic framework arrives just as commercial platforms like Meta's Pocket and DEPIN Studios launch text-to-game tools, suggesting the industry is moving from AI-assisted development to AI-constitutive gameplay faster than many anticipated.
**summary_zh:** 一篇发布在arXiv上的新调查论文《AI-Native Games: A Survey and Roadmap》为"AI原生游戏"建立了严格定义：生成式AI必须是核心玩法循环的构成要素，而不仅仅是生产工具。研究人员分析了53个公开可用的AI原生游戏和原型，提出了G/N分类法——G轴代表玩家面向的游戏类型，N轴代表使生成式AI成为玩法不可或缺的AI机制。研究发现，语料库集中在语言导向设计（叙事冒险、认识论交互）上，而语义裁决、多智能体模拟和生成式构建等类别仍探索不足。论文指出核心设计挑战是"将语义开放性组织成稳定的 gameplay"——这对构建AI驱动体验的开发者是关键洞见。这一学术框架恰逢Meta的Pocket和DEPIN Studios等商业平台推出文本到游戏工具之际发布，表明行业正从AI辅助开发向AI构成性 gameplay 转变，速度比许多人预期的更快。对开发者而言，这意味着AI游戏设计的核心问题不是"能用AI做什么"，而是"如何让AI的输出成为有意义的玩法结构"。
