## Article 4

- **title:** Godot Bans "Vibe-Coded" Contributions, Drawing a Line on AI in Open Source
- **title_zh:** Godot引擎禁止"Vibe Coding"贡献，为开源游戏开发中的AI使用划定红线
- **summary:** The Godot Foundation has enacted strict new rules banning autonomous AI agents and "vibe-coded" pull requests, making it one of the first major open-source game engines to explicitly reject unreviewed AI-generated code. The policy targets a real problem: volunteer maintainers are drowning in low-quality, buggy PRs that cost nothing to submit but hours to review. By requiring new contributors to seek permission for major features and mandating disclosure of even minor AI assistance, Godot is defending a human-centered development culture. This move sets a precedent that other open-source projects may follow, creating a counter-narrative to the "AI everything" trend in commercial game development.
- **summary_zh:** Godot基金会颁布严格新规，禁止自主AI Agent和"Vibe Coded"拉取请求，成为首个明确拒绝未经审核AI生成代码的主流开源游戏引擎。该政策针对的是真实痛点：志愿维护者被零成本提交但耗时审查的低质量、有缺陷的PR淹没。通过要求新贡献者在提交重大功能前获得许可，并强制披露即使是最轻微的AI辅助使用情况，Godot正在捍卫以人为中心的开源开发文化。这一举措为其他开源项目树立了先例，在商业游戏开发"AI万能"趋势之外形成了重要的反叙事。对于依赖Godot的独立开发者而言，这意味着引擎核心将保持人工审核的质量标准，但外围工具和插件生态的AI使用仍不受限制。
