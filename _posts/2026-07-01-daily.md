### 4. Seed Audio 1.0: ByteDance's Unified Audio Scene Generation for Games

**Category:** ai-audio

**English:**
ByteDance has launched Seed Audio 1.0, an audio scene generation model that creates complete audio layers—including dialogue, ambient environment, and sound effects—as a unified output from video input. Unlike siloed audio tools that require separate models for voice, SFX, and ambience, Seed Audio analyzes visual content to produce contextually appropriate audio at $0.18 per minute. The model takes video clips as input, detects settings, motion, and speech cues, then generates synchronized audio designed to drop onto source video without manual alignment. For game developers, this opens possibilities for rapid prototyping of ambient audio layers, procedural environment previews, and automated sound design pipelines. The model is accessible via API and integrates into any video workflow, though it doesn't handle music scoring—that still requires dedicated tools like Suno or Udio.
