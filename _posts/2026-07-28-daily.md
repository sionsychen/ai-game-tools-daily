## Article 1
- **title**: Just Because You Used Claude Doesn't Make It an AI Game
- **title_zh**: 用了 Claude 并不等于做出了 AI 游戏
- **summary**: The piece argues that bolting generative AI onto traditional pipelines for NPC lines or assets does not constitute a true AI game, urging developers to rethink design language rather than treat AI as a cosmetic layer.
- **summary_zh**: 文章指出，仅将生成式 AI 用于批量产出 NPC 台词、背景变体或重复任务文本，并未改变游戏的核心机制。真正的 AI 游戏需要将智能系统融入玩法设计，而不是把 AI 当作廉价的内容填充层。随着市场上“AI 标签”泛滥，开发者和玩家都需要更严格地审视：技术的介入是否真正创造了新的互动体验。

## Article 2
- **title**: Persistent Memory NPCs Are Rewriting Player Agency
- **title_zh**: 持久记忆 NPC 正在重写玩家能动性
- **summary**: AI NPCs with persistent memory can form relationships, hold grudges and recall player choices, but developers must solve consistency, performance and privacy costs before these systems scale.
- **summary_zh**: 具备持久记忆的 AI NPC 让游戏世界能记住玩家的名字、选择与情感互动，使每次游玩都成为独特叙事。然而，记忆系统也带来叙事一致性、计算开销和隐私风险等新挑战。文章认为，真正让 NPC 可信不仅需要先进语言模型，更需要反复的设计打磨与工程优化。
- **sourceUrl**: https://itmunch.com/ai-npcs-changing-gaming-forever/
