## 1. Steam AI Disclosure Hits 20% of Games as Rivals Skip

title: Steam AI Disclosure Hits 20% of Games as Rivals Skip  
title_zh: Steam AI披露率达20%，竞争对手仍按兵不动  
summary: Valve's disclosure rules now cover roughly one in five Steam games, creating a de facto industry standard while Epic and console stores leave AI labels optional.  
summary_zh: Tech-Insider分析显示，Steam约20%游戏已标注AI生成内容，其2026年1月修订版将必须披露的内容限定为玩家实际看到或听到的AI素材，内部效率工具（如AI编程助手）则豁免。由于Steam占据PC数字游戏分销约74%份额，这一规则实际上成为行业准绳。相比之下，Epic Games Store、PlayStation、Xbox、Nintendo eShop及苹果App Store均未设立同等公开机制。对开发者而言，披露本身不带来处罚，但放大了小团队使用生成式工具的成本压力，也让平台权力与透明度的边界再次成为焦点。

## 2. AI Is Democratizing Game Development — But Could It Drown the Indie Scene in Content?

title: AI Democratizes Game Development But Threatens Indie Discoverability  
title_zh: AI降低游戏开发门槛，却让独立游戏 discoverability 更艰难  
summary: Lower production barriers could flood storefronts with AI-assisted games, shifting the indie bottleneck from creation capacity to audience attention and creative taste.  
summary_zh: 文章指出，AI编程、美术与本地化辅助工具让个人开发者和小团队能完成过去需要更多人手的工作，但Steam年发行游戏从2020年的约9654款激增至2025年的逾20000款，已说明供给端正在膨胀。当制作进一步变快，真正的瓶颈将从"能不能做出来"转向"有没有人玩"。在注意力稀缺的环境下，创意总监的品味、社区运营和叙事辨识度将比单纯产出能力更值钱。对独立开发者来说，AI是 democratization 的杠杆，但也会放大 discoverability 危机。

title: Saber Interactive Acknowledges Undisclosed GenAI Use After Writer Accusation  
title_zh: Saber Interactive承认未披露生成式AI使用，前编剧公开指控  
summary: A former lead writer claims Saber replaced her with ChatGPT midway through development, highlighting how undisclosed generative AI use in AAA-adjacent studios is becoming a labor and trust issue.
