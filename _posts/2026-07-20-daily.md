## Steam AI Disclosure Hits 31% of New Releases, Could Reach 50% by 2028

- title: Steam AI Disclosure Hits 31% of New Releases, Could Reach 50% by 2028
- title_zh: Steam 新游 AI 披露率升至 31%，2028 年或过半
- summary: A study of 53,597 Steam releases shows AI-disclosed games now drive 60-90% of platform growth, yet contribute only ~10% of revenue. Lower barriers flood the market with small projects, but commercial success remains concentrated in titles using AI beyond just graphics.
- summary_zh: 对近三年 53,597 款 Steam 新作的研究显示，标注使用生成式 AI 的游戏已占新发布量的约 31%，并贡献了平台 60%-90% 的发行增长；然而这些作品在 2026 年第二季度的收入占比仅约 10%。数据表明，AI 降低了创作门槛，导致大量小体量作品涌入，但真正盈利的仍是将 AI 用于本地化、配音、音乐等多环节，而非仅停留在美术生成的项目。

## Epic Rolls Out AI-Powered Voice NPCs for 36 Fortnite Characters in UEFN

- title: Epic Rolls Out AI-Powered Voice NPCs for 36 Fortnite Characters in UEFN
- title_zh: Epic 为 36 名 Fortnite 角色推出 AI 语音 NPC
- summary: Epic's new UEFN "conversations" system uses Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite and ElevenLabs to let creators build unscripted, memory-enabled NPCs with ~700ms latency. It highlights both the democratization of dynamic AI NPCs and ongoing labor and safety tensions around generative voice.
- summary_zh: Epic 将于 7 月 30 日在 UEFN 全面推出「Conversations」系统，让创作者通过提示词为 36 个 Fortnite 角色（如 Agent Jones、Fishstick）赋予个性、知识与实时对话能力。系统采用 Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite 处理语音输入并生成文本，ElevenLabs 负责语音合成，多人测试延迟约 700 毫秒。这一举措在降低动态 AI NPC 创作门槛的同时，也再次触及声音演员权益、内容安全与 AI 幻觉等行业敏感议题。
- sourceUrl: https://nosmokesport.com/game-news/epic-games-ai-voices-fortnite-creator-islands-july-2026/
- category: ai-npc

## Generative AI in Game Development: Optimizing Pipeline or Risking Craft?

- title: Generative AI in Game Development: Optimizing Pipeline or Risking Craft?
- title_zh: 生成式 AI 进入游戏开发：优化管线还是侵蚀创作？
- summary: PCQuest's analysis argues generative AI's real value lies in backend optimization—localization, QA, and code boilerplate—not fully automated creation. It warns that vibe coding creates architectural debt, AI NPCs introduce 600-1,500ms latency, and unedited AI assets carry copyright risk.
- summary_zh: 本文指出，生成式 AI 在游戏产业的真正价值并非取代创作者，而是优化后端管线——本地化、回归测试、代码模板和动作捕捉清理等。文章同时警告：过度依赖 AI 写代码会造成「Vibe Coding」式架构债务，AI NPC 实时对话带来 600-1500 毫秒延迟，且未经人工编辑的生成资产在美国版权局现行指导下难以获得版权保护。EA 与 Larian 的对比案例表明，未来主流模式是「混合工作室」：人类主导创意，AI 负责可验证的机械性工作。
- sourceUrl: https://www.pcquest.com/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai-in-game-development-optimizing-the-pipeline-safeguarding-the-craft-12160415
